## Video direction

**Ground rule.** Every frame is built on a real captured screenshot of the site, never a rebuild.
The camera moves over the plate; the page itself never animates. The one exception is overlay
chrome the page doesn't have (the round counter in Frame 3, the lockup in Frame 8).

**Camera.** One slow push per frame, 3–5% over the frame's length, always toward the payload.
Never zoom out. Never pan and push at once except Frame 7's deliberate lateral drift. Frames 5
and 6 hide their cut inside a continuing push so two screenshots read as one move.

**Type.** Overlay type is rare and only for what the page cannot say itself. When it appears it
is Inter 900 italic uppercase, tight tracking, or JetBrains Mono for figures — the site's own
voice. No captions burned in; the narration carries the words.

**Color.** The plate supplies the palette. The only added color is the brand red `#ff3e3e` for a
rule, an underline, or the AMS2 mark. Backgrounds stay `#050505`.

**Light.** A 45% vignette on every frame so the screenshot's edges sink and the centre reads.
Spotlight masks (Frames 4–6) lift the target region to full while the rest sits at 55% — they
guide the eye to what the voice is naming, and always clear before the line ends.

**Restraint.** No transitions other than hard cuts. No whooshes. No motion that the voice hasn't
asked for. The site is a telemetry screen and the video should feel like one.
